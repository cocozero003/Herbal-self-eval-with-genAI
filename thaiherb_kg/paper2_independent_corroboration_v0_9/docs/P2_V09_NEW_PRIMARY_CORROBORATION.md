# Paper 2 v0.9: claim-specific independent corroboration

Two *Zingiber officinale* statements (CA001 6-gingerol and CA002 6-shogaol) have corroborating compound-specific measurements in original study Table 2, Koch et al. (2017); 268.3 and 133.2 mg/kg respectively in the documented Japanese ginger rhizome sample. Zingiberene CA005 occurs in this same article abstract at 37.9% of the analyzed terpene composition, which is **not** 37.9% rhizome weight. CA011 *Piper nigrum* piperine: Santosh et al. (2005), RP-HPLC fruit assay 4.5% in the reported study sample, abstract evidence only. These represent **four new corroboration records**, NOT reassessment of the originally archived source papers.

**Research distinction:** These publications were discovered after the existing graph/model development. Although different from the frozen source records, they are not eligible as a prespecified prospective source-disjoint benchmark. Do not back-propagate new sources into baseline training or produce external validation accuracy from these records. No clinical safety/efficacy or product interchangeability follows. Material, chemotype and sample variability limit generalization.

The source references are recorded with DOI, analytical scope, reported values and exact available locator in `results/P2_INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION_v09.csv`; the 100 frozen claim IDs and original source classifications are preserved.
