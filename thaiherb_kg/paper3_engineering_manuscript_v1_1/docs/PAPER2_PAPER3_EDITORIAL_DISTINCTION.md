# Editorial non-overlap note: Paper 2 versus Paper 3

Paper 2 v1.4 is an embedding robustness study of 20 herbs and 10 formula documents, evaluated for seed repeatability, perturbation, formula holdout, and source sensitivity. It contains 74 source-reviewed formula memberships in its later freeze.

Paper 3 v1.1 is an RDF representation/provenance and SHACL method study on a separate **frozen 208-assertion V3 source corpus** with 71 original formula membership claims, 100 chemistry, 30 pharmacology target, and seven clinical records. Paper 3 uses no Paper 2 embedding AP, AUROC or seed-stability numbers as original Paper 3 results.

The two papers share project provenance and some herb/formula entities but differ in unit of analysis, primary endpoint, datasets' exact frozen versions, and method. Authors should disclose overlap of source material and not claim each graph record as an independent biological study.

Paper 3 claims are *representation recoverability* and *SHACL structural conformance*. Neither article asserts independent biological validity without expert gold.
