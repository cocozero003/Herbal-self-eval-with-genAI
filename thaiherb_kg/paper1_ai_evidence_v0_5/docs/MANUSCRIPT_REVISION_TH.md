# Paper 1 manuscript editorial decisions — v0.5

- Archived values in Table 2–4 remain historical experiment results. Do not substitute v0.5 experimental cosine/rank outputs in those tables without access to archived 19-class mapping and archived walk corpora.
- v0.3 deletion of F05/ginger, F05/Java long pepper and F05/black pepper is invalid: F05 continues on printed page 162; do not cite v0.3 as primary source correction.
- Revised chemistry curation is a new candidate 19-class taxonomy. It is not original curation. In the article label the experiment `Post-hoc sensitivity to a chemically better-differentiated candidate taxonomy`.
- Add 30-row AI-assisted evidence ledger as supplement; clearly identify which assertions are based on primary isolation, secondary reviews, or government monographs. AI is neither an independent blinded expert nor a replacement for source-specific review.
- Evidence of a compound occurring in a plant is not evidence of clinical efficacy or herb interchangeability. Species, plant part, preparation, stereochemistry and sample conditions remain qualifiers.
- Published repeatability values are not biological validation. Include external expert protocol and genuinely independent gold only after collected.
- Update Data Availability to cite the Drive release and GitHub branch, checksum, scripts, and missing original class/vector archive; avoid claiming full reproduction of the original experiment.
- The appendix draft document in manuscript/ includes these caveats but is not submission-ready.
